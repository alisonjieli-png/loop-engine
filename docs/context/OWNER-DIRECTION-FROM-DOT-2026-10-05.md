## Review material

Two review banks answer the owner's October 5 request for personas and
adversarial questions about every frame. One holds 2,880 synthetic reviewer
profiles and 3,720 question records. The other holds 2,400 reviewer
configurations and 3,215 probes built from 120 criteria in 12 domains, 120
domain challenges and five evidence protocols: falsify, detect, boundary,
repair and transfer. Its assessments use pass, fail, unknown and not
applicable, with anchored grades from 0 to 4 that are never averaged and never
fill missing evidence. Frame coverage has five separate measures: indexed,
decoded, mechanically checked, semantically reviewed and visually sampled.

The criteria, protocols, statuses, grades, coverage measures and persona axes
generalize to any film, render, package or report. Gameplay and data-identity
criteria generalize with light editing. Example profiles, inventory routing
and the receipts for named films and games are project-specific and stay out
of the library. A third record, an evidence-and-learning ledger with 13
lessons and 2,162 items still to review, exists only in Dot's own storage.

1. Use the ledger as a supply source with one candidate per distinct job (S-6.217).
2. Add the review family as components: evidence protocols, statuses, anchored grades, frame-coverage measures and honest synthetic personas, first applied to the creative proof.
3. State the creative acceptance rules: finished videos of 60 seconds or more, labeled previews, measured alignment and platform safe zones, phone-size playback, sound, HTML/CSS text layers and native vertical resolution.
4. Record gameplay through a connected desktop, label emulation separately from real play, and keep regression checks that fail when required geometry is missing.
5. Extend the keyword-multiplication plan of S-6.215 with null branches, no dropped searches, time-to-live refresh, starvation-free rotation, depth controls, result lineage and generated follow-ups.
6. Keep differing values as dated alternatives and keep restricted material separately marked instead of deleting it.
7. Give long-running supply lines hourly, six-hourly and daily supervisor reviews that tell a stall from a healthy long job.
8. Generate host formats from one canonical capability and test what survives each conversion; expose reviewed plugin operations rather than a generic executor; enforce per-action approval in the adapter.
9. Reconcile Dot's Public Good query bank (180 tasks, 1,440 planned evidence cards, 16 blueprints), now located in the owner's Drive, with S-6.216.
