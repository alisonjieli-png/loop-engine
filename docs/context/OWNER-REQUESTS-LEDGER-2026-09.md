Kind: dated ledger of the owner's requests, one row for each request, kept
as a context record. It quotes only what a repository document records, names
the roadmap steps that answer each request, and says where each one stands
today. The [roadmap](../roadmap/roadmap.yaml) remains the only task
authority; this ledger checks whether the roadmap and the live service answer
what the owner asked, and it grants nothing. The owner's standing rules are in
the [commit, push and release authority](../../AGENTS.md#commit-push-and-release-authority)
section of AGENTS.md, which this ledger does not restate.

The owner, September 26, 2026, in the message that started this ledger: "I
feel like you skipped a lot of my prompts and next steps". The ledger exists
so that no request is skipped silently: a request is either answered live, or
its gap is written down.

## How to read the table

- One row is one request, in date order. Where one message asked for several
  things that different steps answer, each thing has its own row.
- "The owner's words" quotes the recorded words. Where a handoff recorded a
  summary rather than the words, the cell says so.
- "Source" names the document that records the request: a dated handoff
  (`handoff 09-24` is `SESSION-HANDOFF-2026-09-24.md`), the
  [ninety-day plan](NINETY-DAY-PLAN-2026-09-25.md), the
  [decision table](../../AGENTS.md#decisions-that-stand-until-the-owner-changes-them)
  or the authority section of AGENTS.md, or the evidence field of a roadmap
  step that quotes the owner.
- "Roadmap steps" names the steps that answer the request, or `none`.
- "State today" is one of six words: `live` (a customer or a person can use
  it on the live service), `on main` (merged and pushed, not released, or a
  rule or record that needs no release), `building` (a line is committed or
  running), `proposed` (a roadmap step exists and no build has started),
  `blocked` (waits on a named condition) or `not started` (no step and no
  build).
- "Gap" says in one sentence what is missing when the state is not live.
- Rows R-01 to R-96 were written on September 26, 2026 at 12:45 UTC from
  the documents on `main` at `76e14faa`. Rows R-97 to R-110 were added at
  16:45 UTC the same day, after a sweep of every quoted owner passage in the
  same sources found requests the first pass had not listed; they follow the
  first pass's rows in their own date order, so that a request keeps its
  identifier between the two dated records of the day. Every state was judged
  again at 16:45 UTC from `main` at `43b421f8`, the release records and

- One row, R-97, is dated September 21, 2026: the September 22 handoff and

## How this ledger is checked

Current behaviour, on `main` with this record:

- [`tools/owner_requests_ledger.py`](../../tools/owner_requests_ledger.py)
  reads the table above and the roadmap. It reports every request whose named
  steps are not live in the roadmap (a live step is `live_qualified` or
  `published`), every request whose state above is not `live`, every request
  with no step, every roadmap step that no request names, and every step
  identifier above that the roadmap does not know. It writes a dated record
  under [`artifacts/owner-requests/`](../../artifacts/owner-requests/README.md)
  (record type `owner_requests_report/v1`, never overwriting an earlier file
  of the same day) and prints a plain table. With `--summary PATH` it also
  writes a Markdown summary, and with `--check` it writes nothing and fails
  on an unknown step identifier or a malformed row.
- [`tools/test_owner_requests_ledger.py`](../../tools/test_owner_requests_ledger.py)
  runs the tool on a fixture ledger and a fixture roadmap, fails it on a
  known-wrong ledger that names a step the roadmap lacks, and checks that
  every step identifier in this table exists in the committed roadmap.

Planned behaviour, proposed and not installed: a cron entry runs the tool
every day at 06:30 UTC, writes the day's record under
`$HOME/.le-ci-tmp/owner-requests/records/` and, because the artifact board
that a session publishes to is never reachable from cron, always writes the
Markdown summary to `$HOME/.le-ci-tmp/owner-requests/latest.md`. A session
copies a record it wants to keep into `artifacts/owner-requests/` under its
dated name. The entry is written out in the
[record folder's README](../../artifacts/owner-requests/README.md).

## What this ledger does not do

- It does not change the roadmap. A state above that disagrees with a step's
  roadmap status (for example a live page whose step is `offline_verified`)
  is a finding for the integrating session, listed by the tool.
- It does not quote private notes outside the repository. The owner's
  requests before September 21, 2026 (for example "NEVER tell me to rotate
  or revoke an API key" of September 19 and 21, and "stop asking me to push,
  when you fix something you push!" of September 2) are in the authority
  section of AGENTS.md and the earlier dated handoffs.
- A sweep of every quoted passage in the sources, with wrapped lines joined,
  is how the second pass found rows R-97 to R-110; a quote that spans two
  lines of a handoff is missed by a search that reads one line at a time.
- It records no approval, budget or permission.
