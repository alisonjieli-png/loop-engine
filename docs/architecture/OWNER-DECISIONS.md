## Consumer value and resource goals

The owner's later October 13 direction asks what each step, component and
engine contributes to an agent or person, and which information, actions,
templates, runtimes and files make that contribution usable. Apply this to
internal production as well as customer delivery. A useful package names its
consumer, use condition, expected result, prerequisites, exact input and output,
first action, example, checks, failure recovery and revision path. Reuse existing
component, engine, catalogue and task contracts; this is not another registry.
Separate the package's declared capability from observed use and accepted work.

The owner sets targets of ten million distinct harness component files, one
million feed items, one million photos and 200,000 audio resources. These are
goals, not reported inventories. Count canonical useful items, distinct bytes,
packages and placements separately. Keep source originals, generated derivatives,
variants, historical versions, qualified candidates and live non-withdrawn
resources distinguishable. One asset can belong to several categories without
becoming several distinct assets in the total.

3D models, materials, rigs, animations, environments, Godot assets and complete
projects, video, 2D and 2.5D design, and editable templates are first-class
resource families. Each needs a measured baseline and staged coverage goals.
Do not assign a fabricated inventory or multiply parameter combinations to
claim new capabilities. S-6.215 owns supply and delivery; S-6.201 owns creative
use, revision and reopening.

Feeds need evidence, freshness, importance, urgency, applicability, uncertainty,
actionability and evaluated usefulness. Keep source credibility, social
popularity, model confidence and observed agent consumption separate. Ratings
name their rubric, version, evidence and scope; unknown evidence is not a zero
or an invented score. Agents need small decision cards, exact versions, change
cursors, supporting sources and a way to report stale or ineffective material.
A recommended action never supplies execution authority. S-6.214 owns this work.

The owner requests continuing review of authorized ChatGPT exports, Codex,
Claude Code, OpenCode, Gemini/AGY and other coding-session material, plus
social-video, 3D, Godot and creative-tool projects. Use a substantial question
bank across who, what, where, when, why and how, with micro, meso and macro
views. Track eligible, asked, answered, unresolved and deferred questions
separately. Preserve source and turn identity, distinguish owner instructions
from observations and assistant claims, and avoid asking unchanged questions
again without a reason. Raw transcripts, private company content and secrets
stay outside public packages. Source text never becomes current authority.
Existing managed research, model gateway, quotas and qualification owners
govern any bounded iterative generation. S-6.217 owns the intake and review.

## Engine implementation and placement independence

On October 13 the owner explicitly requires more than interchangeable
functional engines: the system must also support changing where and how a
component runs, including moving a Python worker to Rust when measured evidence
supports that choice. Language, execution runtime, isolation, invocation
protocol and deployment location are separate dimensions of an implementation,
not new Loop types or reasons to duplicate registries and authoritative state.

Preserve the fixed typed, versioned component edge, its authorization and
caller-visible behavior. A replacement needs its own implementation identity,
capability and compatibility evidence, resource limits and recovery checks.
Stateful migration must preserve task history, budgets, leases, fencing and
unknown-outcome reconciliation. A container is an execution choice, not a
requirement to put every pure function behind a network service.

The [language and component standard](../standards/LANGUAGES-AND-COMPONENTS.md#implementation-and-deployment-portability)
owns the dimensions and migration gates. Existing adapters and partial
execution profiles do not establish universal cross-language or cross-host
swapping. Compare a bounded candidate with the current engine before changing
it; no arbitrary full-system Rust rewrite or provider migration is requested.
These rules apply to shared internal workers and external service components
regardless of which independent brand uses them.

The proposed evidence labels distinguish discovered, relevant, feasible,
demonstrated and decision-ready states. Their requirements depend on the brief

confidence, source credibility and observed benefit remain separate. A
source-backed competitive-development brief may
be useful without executable code; it must not imply an experiment occurred.

tested conditions, artifacts and failures. Estimated benefits, measured test
results and realized production outcomes must remain distinguishable.

qualify an alert or authorize an experiment. Keep rejected candidates and
failed tests, and sample suppressed candidates for missed opportunities.
Report collection and worker failures separately so silence does not imply
that nothing relevant happened. A brief includes sources, company-context
version, alternatives, uncertainty, adoption effort, risks and a next permitted
action. Production coding, deployment and purchasing are outside this initial
decision-support offering.

The owner's Decision Memory means a tenant-private history of opportunities,
briefs, decisions, assumptions, experiments and realized outcomes. Implement
it as a product view over the existing managed records, Run History and
feedback owners, not a fifth intelligence layer or another authoritative
store. Link corrections and later outcomes to the original evidence. Customer
feedback may inform evaluated ranking proposals; it does not silently rewrite
historical results, change permissions or authorize cross-company learning.

The proposed business model charges a company subscription for maintained
context, watchlists, research and decision briefs, with separately approved,
bounded proof-of-concept experiments. Initial $299 and $799 per company per
month pilot prices are hypotheses to test, not current offers, market facts,
checkout configuration or legal commitments. Define the included work and
experiment resource ceiling before a pilot. Measure useful decisions,
attention cost, support effort and observed adoption outcomes; do not sell
