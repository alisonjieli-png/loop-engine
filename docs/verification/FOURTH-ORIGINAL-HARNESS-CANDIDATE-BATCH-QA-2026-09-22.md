| QA finding in initial bytes | Repair in current bytes and discriminating fixture |
|---|---|
| The stock pool excluded reserved units, but order demand did not; a previously reserved unit could be promised twice. | Compute free pool as gross on-hand less holds and its active reservations. Compute each order's incremental demand as ordered less fulfilled, cancelled and already reserved units. With gross 12 and 4 reserved for A, only 8 free remain; if A and B each ordered 10 under A-first priority, new allocation is at most 6 for A and 2 for B. Missing reservation-to-order links block the proposal. |
| Confirmed inbound could satisfy an order whose promise preceded receipt. | Keep inbound in a separate stock-ready-time pool. Compare every new **and existing** future reservation against the order's required stock-ready time, derived with handling or transit lead time. Stock ready on the 12th cannot count toward an on-time promise requiring readiness on the 10th. A late reservation remains committed pending authorized release. |
| Return credit checked unit count but missed an earlier partial monetary credit. | Keep separate unit and amount headroom for each exact original sale line. A unit originally eligible for 100 currency units with an earlier 40-unit partial credit permits at most 60 further credit, even when its unit count still permits one return. Unknown prior-credit linkage blocks the proposal. |
| Project variance linked a commitment to an invoice but did not clear an accrual when the actual posted. | Match cost events through purchase order, receipt, accrual, reversal and invoice. For an 8,000 commitment and 8,000 accrual later replaced by an 8,000 posted invoice and 8,000 accrual reversal, known exposure remains 8,000: actual 8,000 plus open accrual 0 plus residual commitment 0. If the invoice-to-accrual clearing link is missing, withhold a final variance. |
