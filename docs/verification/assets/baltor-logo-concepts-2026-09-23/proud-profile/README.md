# Proud husky profile variations

The owner's later references changed the direction from a smiling frontal
mascot to a mature dog with a lifted chin, longer muzzle and upright chest.
The owner prefers two-tone and negative-space designs and also asked for
different stroke styles. Small-size clarity remains a requirement.

Open the [browser comparison](index.html). It shows actual 16- and 32-pixel
SVGs first, on light and dark surfaces, plus browser-tab and header examples.
Enlarged geometry and larger image studies are optional sections.

| Family | 16-pixel SVG | 32-pixel SVG | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curved profile | [16](../micro-marks/soft-16.svg) | [32](../micro-marks/soft-32.svg) | Longer muzzle, upright neck and white cheek cut. Omits the summit at tiny size. |
| Summit profile | [16](../micro-marks/peak-mask-16-mono.svg) | [32](../micro-marks/peak-mask-32-mono.svg) | More angular profile with a broad summit ledge; monochrome adaptation of the color study. |
| Single-cut silhouette | [16](../micro-marks/single-cut-16.svg) | [32](../micro-marks/single-cut-32.svg) | Two filled paths, with eyes and inner-ear detail removed. |

Each size is drawn separately. Explicit inverse files use a white silhouette
and navy cuts for the dark surface in the preview. These are original vector
studies with no raster embedding, gradients, strokes or filters. Breed
recognition at 16 pixels remains uncertain even when the silhouette is clear.
The white cuts are real filled shapes; they are not transparent holes.

The page also contains three larger raster identity studies:
[angular ascent](01-angular-ascent.png), [swept negative space](02-swept-negative-space.png)
and [broad brush](03-broad-brush.png). They were produced with the built-in
image generation tool using the supplied reference screenshots. Exact
[prompts](prompts.json) are saved. All three are transparent 1,254-pixel PNGs.
The generated results retain more detail and tonal variation than requested,
especially the brush version, so they are visual references rather than
finished two-color vector masters or favicon files.

The small SVGs were inspected in Chromium at actual display sizes and device
scale factor one. The browser comparison loads all images and has no
horizontal overflow at desktop or phone width. The
[actual-size preview](actual-size-preview.png) and
[expanded geometry preview](geometry-preview.png) preserve the check.
No production logo or website asset was replaced.
