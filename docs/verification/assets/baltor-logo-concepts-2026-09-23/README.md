# Baltor dog and ice logo concepts

The owner rejected these initial vector directions and supplied a new
front-facing husky reference. See the [refined visual concepts](refined-husky/index.html)
and [their design notes](refined-husky/README.md) for the later direction.
These original SVGs remain here as earlier design attempts.

These are four original SVG explorations of the owner's requested geometric
Alaskan racing dog with its front paws on an ice peak. They are design
candidates, not the production logo. No existing website asset was edited.
The fourth uses the lean muzzle and upright-ear direction visible in the
owner's reference strip. Its geometry is original rather than traced from an
individual photograph or emblem.

Open [the contact sheet](contact-sheet.html) or its
[rendered preview](contact-sheet.png) to compare each mark on light and dark
surfaces at 128, 32 and 16 pixels. The forward-facing concept has an explicit
[one-ink rendering](02-frontier-face-one-ink.svg). The grayscale checks on
the other concepts are tonal previews, not finished one-ink logos.

| Concept | Intended strength | Current concern |
|---|---|---|
| [Summit stance](01-summit-stance.svg) and its [reversed version](01-summit-stance-dark.svg) | Distinct transparent dog and ice forms; suitable beside a wordmark. | The hosting interface must select the light or reversed asset for its actual surface. |
| [Frontier face](02-frontier-face.svg) | Symmetric face reads quickly inside the current square brand position. | Ear and face facets may merge below 32 pixels. |
| [Floe runner](03-floe-runner.svg) | Strong silhouette and racing motion in a square tile. | It resembles the first side-profile concept at a glance; test one at a time with users. |
| [Ridge husky](04-ridge-husky.svg) | Leaner muzzle and uphill athletic profile with both front paws on the ice slope. | The thin leg gaps may close at 16 pixels. |

The existing website uses a 32-pixel rounded brand tile, neutral surfaces,
and one principal blue action color. The concepts use that blue with ice
tones and a dark ink. Production work should choose one mark, adjust its
optical spacing beside the Baltor wordmark, make an explicit one-ink version,
and test it in the actual header and browser tab in both appearance modes.
SVG titles and descriptions are present; a linked logo should still have an
accessible text name in its surrounding link.

Six SVG files, including the reversed colorway of the first concept and the
one-ink rendering of the second concept, parse
as standalone SVG. All 20 image references in the contact sheet resolve. A
headless Chromium rendering was inspected at 1280 by 900 pixels. At 32
pixels, the forward-facing tile has the clearest face. At 16 pixels, the
full-body dog marks lose detail, so a chosen full-body mark would need a
separate simplified favicon before production use.
