# Summit profile micro mark

Original vector study by Codex, September 23, 2026. Draft for comparison.
The filenames preserve the assigned variation identity; the direction changed
from a frontal mask to a lifted profile after the owner supplied the mature
champion dog references.

- [16 pixel source](peak-mask-16.svg): four filled paths, a short upright ear,
  long muzzle, white chest and one blue ledge. Omits paws and fur cuts.
- [32 pixel source](peak-mask-32.svg): four filled paths, a longer lifted muzzle
  and a curved white throat. Omits paws and full body detail.

Both are original geometry drawn for their native viewBox and display size.
There are no raster images, filters, gradients, external assets or fonts.
The 32 pixel drawing is independently spaced rather than a doubled 16 pixel
image. The palette is navy `#0B2E55`, white and ice blue `#58C4E6`.

Chromium rendered both at their actual CSS size and device pixel ratio one;
Codex inspected the resulting screenshot beside four times enlarged vectors.
The profile and ice cue remain distinguishable at 16 pixels. Breed detail is
limited there: the mark reads as an upright canine rather than identifying
an Alaskan racing dog precisely. The 32 pixel variant has a clearer lifted
chin and curved chest. The angular ear still gives it some wolf character.
These sources are designed for a pale surface; they need a separate reversed
palette on dark surfaces. This is visual inspection, not a user recognition
test or an approved brand mark.
