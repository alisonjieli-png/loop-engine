# Lifted muzzle variation

The [16 pixel file](soft-16.svg) and [32 pixel file](soft-32.svg) follow the
owner's newer reference: a mature dog with a lifted chin and upright chest.
A long muzzle and a white cheek that continues into the chest are the main
recognition cues. The expression stays neutral. Each drawing uses navy and
white with a transparent outer background and its own native coordinate grid.

The earlier [16 pixel frontal attempt](soft-frontal-16.svg) and
[32 pixel frontal attempt](soft-frontal-32.svg) remain as discarded studies.
Their round faces and large features read too young for the new direction.

The new pair deliberately omits paws and the summit at these small sizes.
Those elements belong in the larger companion emblem. At 16 pixels the dog
can still read as a wolf or fox, and breed recognition is not established.
Diagonal and curved boundaries still receive browser antialiasing. The navy
outer silhouette needs a light surrounding surface to remain visible.

These are original editable SVG studies. No production asset is replaced.
