# Alternate husky favicon pair

These original SVG drawings use native 16 and 32 pixel coordinate grids.
They preserve two ears, a broad white face mask and one solid navy summit.
The mask suggests a husky without drawing eyes, a nose or fur. Both marks
have transparent backgrounds and are intended for a light surface.

[16 pixel mark](alternate-16.svg) uses three flat shapes. One pixel of space
separates the head and summit. There are no paws at this size.

[32 pixel mark](alternate-32.svg) keeps the same three-shape construction
with a two-pixel separation. A first draft added white paw cutouts to the
summit. A browser render showed these looking like gaps in a tower, so the
final alternate omits them. This direction prioritizes the head and summit.

The main uncertainty is recognition: without eyes or a nose, the face can
also read as a fox or wolf mask. Judge that at actual size beside the other
directions. Ordinary antialiasing remains on diagonal edges; there is no
blur filter, gradient, stroke or shadow.

The actual-size Chromium render at a display scale of one showed distinct
ears, a white angular mask and a separate summit at both sizes. It also
showed that the 16 pixel head can read as a cat or helmet. This makes it a
useful comparison, rather than a selected brand mark.

The navy fill needs an inverse treatment for a dark surface. These are
local design candidates. They are not deployed brand assets.
