## Present operating state

Fly release 15 from checked revision a51ac963 is live. The
[saved release record](../../artifacts/architecture-audit-2026-09-19/pilot-release-13.json)
names its image and rollback target and reports its guarded deployment,
readiness, grants, 34 offered starter items, nine withheld items, and
post-deploy website and service checks. The
[independent public audit](../verification/SAAS-LIVE-READINESS-AND-CUSTOMER-JOURNEY-2026-09-22.md)
found the same homepage bytes on eight hostnames. The invitation list is
open; public registration, sign-up email, checkout and portal remain off.
There is no saved invited-person path from live download through native
harness use to accepted work. The demo, examples, docs and status subdomains
still serve the main homepage, and the root HEAD request returns 404.
The privacy page is live; the terms are a draft requiring the owner's
decision. These are distinct, dated observations, not an assertion that
the site is ready to charge a customer.

The current owner decision is one Baltor Pro plan at 29 United States
dollars per month, free search, one downloaded item as the measured unit,
no launch overage and a free invited beta entitlement. The service does
not pay the customer's model provider. A multi-file package should be one
downloaded item when its required members arrive together; the
competitor and economics report (kept in the owner's private research folder, outside this public repository)
marks that as a contract interpretation to prove. The roadmap now routes
free search through bounded allowance and protects exact downloads behind
the applicable entitlement. Do not conflate a subscription session
creation with a completed charge or paid access.

## Release and operations gates

    Reviewed exact source tree
    ├── Committed revision with continuous integration passed
    ├── Built image digest and compatible host record
    ├── Deployment with readiness and packaged grants
    ├── Full website, catalogue, service and protocol checks on every host
    ├── Saved active and rollback image, host-file and catalogue identities
    └── Monitored customer path, withdrawal and recovery

This is D-18 and S-6.35 work. The automatic grant step ran in release 15;
the workflow still needs the full per-host suites and release record as
automated post-deploy gates. Keep failed attempts and their successors.
An older image must refuse a newer incompatible state record. Withdrawal
and key revocation must survive rollback. A check that removes the grant
step must fail. The [current deployment map](../architecture/MVP-CLIENT-SERVER.md#current-deployment)
remains the source for the actual host, not a proposed diagram.

## First customer and paid journey

| Stage | Minimum observed proof before saying it works |
|---|---|
| Invitation | A visitor reaches one visible form from the primary action, submits once, sees confirmation, and an operator can read the exact request. The live [interface review](../verification/LIVE-UI-UX-AND-ACQUISITION-REVIEW-2026-09-22.md) found a two-click waitlist path and phone actions below the first viewport. |
| Account and key | An invited person outside engineering confirms mailbox control, signs in without help, creates a personal key, connects a supported client, sees usage, revokes access and observes the next request refused. |
| Native task | The client searches, fetches exact approved bytes, places them at qualified paths in a fresh isolated harness, records discovery, actual use and an independently accepted result. Fetched is not loaded, and loaded is not used. |
| Payment | A test customer completes checkout, signed event reconciliation, entitlement, billing portal and cancellation, then loses paid access at the correct boundary. Terms and the privacy promise must match the running code. |
| Account lifecycle | Recovery, support, export and deletion work with tenant isolation, including after subscription cancellation. |

The [draft redesign review](../verification/DRAFT-HOMEPAGE-UX-REVIEW-2026-09-23.md)
found that the in-flight page improves the invitation route and labels
recorded versus illustrated behavior, but its right-hand demonstration
grew and the primary action and form remain below the initial phone
viewport. Those measurements belong to S-6.33 before deployment.

## Library scale and service capacity

The owner target is 10,000 then 100,000 distinct active approved packages,
with unique eligible files reported separately. The existing host body
format is one ordinary Markdown string per item. New 80 original Agent
Skill drafts and nine mixed-format drafts are candidate-only. Their static
quality checks are not independent admission, customer rights or useful
task evidence.

The [100,000-row offline probe](../../artifacts/hundredk-serving-probe-2026-09-22/README.md)
found a 122-megabyte synthetic manifest against a two-megabyte loader
limit, a 73-megabyte bare list against a 262-kilobyte response ceiling, a
47-megabyte tenant grant array and a 48.57-second, 2.10-gibibyte current
Retriever rebuild per request. The staged release, sparse-grant,
persistent-index, bounded-list and complete-package contracts under
S-6.62, S-6.32 and D-05 must be proven before a size claim. Measure
source-to-distinct-method yield, review errors, model and human cost,
file-size distribution, native pickup, search quality and withdrawal
latency on the first 1,000 real candidates, then at 10,000 and 100,000.
Do not count synthetic rows, copied client layouts or rejected drafts as
customer supply.

## Acquisition and business proof

The proposed paid message is a focused task result with visible source,
rights, effects, selected native files and an independent check, not a raw
catalogue count. Native skill installers and large directories already
offer alternatives. The first useful conversion is an invited visitor
who reaches an accepted step and repeats it. Before broad paid traffic,
prove the direct invitation path, complete account and native journey,
honest current versus illustrated page copy, a support route, and a
matched outcome example. Then test a small capped channel with
first-party counts for visit, invitation, account, key, fetched,
loaded, accepted and retained paying customer. The public privacy
notice must describe any new campaign data collected. Compare
contribution after processing, index, transfer, storage, review, refresh,
support and refunds with customer acquisition cost and retention. The
market report (kept in the owner's private research folder, outside this public repository)
offers four tests that could refute the paid advantage; no willingness to
pay has been measured here.
