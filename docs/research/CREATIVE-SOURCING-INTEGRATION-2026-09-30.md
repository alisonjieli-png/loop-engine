## Decisions for Baltor

Keep discovery, source review, candidate preparation, execution and publication
separate. The reported 806 skill paths are discovery matches, not admitted
packages. The reported pose-selection experiment uses a controlled population;
it does not qualify selection among real generated images. Preserve these limits
when the underlying files are recovered. Do not copy another project's runtime,
approval system or private histories into Baltor.

The strongest product pattern is one editable implementation that can produce
a short explanation, a browser interaction and reusable customer files. Keep
original geometry, timings, facts and controls. A new colour or camera preset
does not count as another implementation. Use the existing Loop runtime and
typed engine slots, with pinned third-party adapters and a qualified native
alternative or a recorded reason why none is needed.

Start with a factual episode using synthetic data, then a rigged presenter.
The proposed Denominator Trap is a suitable first brief because its arithmetic
can be independently checked without acquiring external footage. The presenter
proof must use actual narration if it claims speech synchronization. The current
marketing exports are silent still-frame compositions; they do not establish
gesture, lip-sync or collision accuracy.

Retain the other proposals as distinct leads: Map Mystery, Actually Explained,
Signal School, train and landmark puzzles, Streetprint, One Drop's Journey,
Motion Under the Shell, Tiny World Trials, Object Afterlife, City Under the
Surface, Inside the Loop, revision-aware assembly instructions and product-rule
puzzles. These names are working labels, not cleared trademarks. The named
Venus, Voyager, sea-ice, flight, underwater-signal and astrolabe scripts need
their complete source files and claim-level review before production.

## Quality checks before publication

Turn the reported renderer defects into reproducible tests once its source
revision is available. A passing manifest is insufficient:

- Measure visible motion over every interval that requires it. Reject the
  known-wrong clip that changes once and then freezes. Deliberate title-card
  holds and still-frame marketing drafts need their own declared expectations.
- Inspect rendered safe areas and actual text bounds. Reject a clip with
  clipped text even when its metadata says the layout is safe.
- Detect contacts or visual events in frames and sound onsets in decoded audio
  independently. Report measured offsets and uncertainty. A shared schedule
  cannot supply its own synchronization measurement.
- Compare equivalent times across frame rates, retain persistent rig joints,
  check hand/neck visibility and collisions, then revise and reopen the native
  project. A transparent preview alone does not qualify the final alpha export.

Keep all attempts, rejected outputs and manual repair time. Freeze the same
brief, resources and acceptance checks for renderer and with/without-Baltor
comparisons. The same frame or passive asset does not require a separate model
call or executable graph vertex.
