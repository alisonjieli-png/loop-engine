## Ambitions that are not established current product capabilities

The following are research or implementation targets in the fully reviewed main specifications. Their inclusion in a mandate is not execution evidence.

- **Adaptive composition of solver teams.** Joint choice of question graphs, context, model settings, candidate Solution portfolios, independent verifiers, search strategies and scheduling. Current public solving remains a staged coordinator with shared model services; generic delegation and reactive mechanics do not prove an autonomous adaptive peer mesh.
- **Complete task-question graph compilation.** Conditional persona/situation expansion, semantic deduplication, conflation repair, lazy question activation and decision-linked stopping require observable runtime integration and value tests. Fully read annex F is a source design for this ambition, not another engine to instantiate.
- **Broad external research.** Browser interaction, bounded crawling, archives, monitoring, source extraction, citation verification and research-to-intelligence are distinct possible operations. Existing Web Research adapters and references do not establish the complete source-to-reviewed-intelligence system.
- **General distributed and service work.** Remote workers, Kubernetes placement, durable detached ownership, leases, join policies, cancellation and cross-store recovery require separate proofs. The same Loop runtime can own these operations without asserting all placements are implemented.
- **Repeated-task learning and runtime allocation.** History-conditioned questions, context, verification, model/harness routing and computation allocation require valid matched comparisons, applicability limits and negative-transfer evidence. Current record/projection plumbing and offline fixtures do not establish general benefit.
- **Kaggle recursive improvement.** Absolute:4186 defines levels from one-competition evolution through cross-competition memory, Practitioner/search-policy improvement, curriculum/evaluator improvement, trained routers/adapters and meta-improvement. Its completion gate at :4745 requires a later competition using reviewed prior intelligence, a held-out transfer measure, negative-transfer controls and independent evaluation of a Practitioner improvement. Current 120-competition metadata access and three-source qualification mechanics do not meet those gates.
- **Broad flagship solution factories.** The specifications target reusable packages for data repair, research, repository modernization, model experimentation, media, simulation and service deployment. Open task intake and small Python/CSV/document fixtures are a narrower current surface.
- **Fair external-harness benchmarking.** The reviewed specification distinguishes model, provider, harness, environment, population, evaluator and budget, and requires exact comparison groups. A source catalog or adapter signature check is not a live head-to-head result.
- **Vendor-model mechanisms.** The master appendix preserves model-card claims with an explicit re-verification warning. No external model specification or claimed architecture mechanism was verified during this bounded audit, and none is repeated here as a fact.

## Useful intent to retain

The strongest recurring ideas remain compatible with the current repository: one Loop runtime; orthogonal role/mode/profile/relationship/authority; passive typed records; one record authority with rebuildable indexes; immutable provenance and loss-aware migrations; scoped private context; independent builder and reviewer authority; explicit external effects; preserved failed trials; and no promotion from retrieval, repetition, confidence or a good score.

The historical text also repeatedly rejects file-presence proof, fixture-as-live claims, unmatched benchmark comparisons and universal conclusions from a successful example. Those standards should be applied to the mandate's own ambitions.
