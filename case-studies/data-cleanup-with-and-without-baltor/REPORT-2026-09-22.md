# Data cleanup with and without Baltor material: report

Kind: dated measurement report for roadmap steps D-07, D-08, S-6.37 and
S-6.47. The design was frozen and committed on September 22, 2026 before the
first model call. The model calls ran from 23:33 on September 22 to 00:32 on
September 23, United States Eastern time. This report does not change the
[frozen design](DESIGN.md#data-cleanup-with-and-without-baltor-material-frozen-design).

Status: completed. Every planned step ran, within the request ceiling.

## Summary

The question was whether an approved Baltor item, placed in a fresh harness
step, raises a small or cheap model's independently checked result on data
cleanup, and whether it closes the gap to a larger model without the item.
On this population the answer is no, and in one family the item made the
result worse.

1. The local small model, `qwen2.5-coder:7b`, could not operate the harness.
   In all 37 of its steps, including the pilot, it wrote its first tool call
   as plain text instead of a structured tool call. Pi therefore treated the
   reply as a final answer and ended the step after one request, with no
   output file. Material in `AGENTS.md` or in the prompt made no difference.
   It scored 0 in every family and arm.
2. The cheap cloud model, `glm-5.3-flash:cloud`, already averaged between
   0.948 and 1.000 in each family without material and passed 12 of 12 steps.
3. With the approved item, the cheap model was clearly worse on phone numbers:
   0.842 in all three repetitions, against 1.000, 0.974 and 1.000 without it.
   On emails, addresses and duplicates it was not separated from its own
   no-material result.
4. The phone item's digit-count method is incomplete, and the cheap model
   followed it instead of its own correct knowledge. It lost every United
   Kingdom number written with a `(0)` trunk prefix, every number dialled with
   the 011 prefix, the area code that starts with 0 and the `+41` number with
   an impossible length, in all three repetitions.
5. With material, the cheap model spent more in every family: 45 to 446
   percent more prompt tokens per step, and more requests and more seconds per
   step.
6. The larger cloud model, `glm-5.2:cloud`, without material was not separated
   from the cheap model without material in any family.
7. Neither claim in the frozen claim rules is supported. Material was clearly
   better in 0 of 4 families and clearly worse in 1. The small model with
   material was clearly worse than the larger model in 4 of 4 families.

Requests used: 225 of the 400 allowed.

## 2. Population and selection rule

The population is synthetic, written by a seeded generator from clean truths
and named corruptions, and disclosed as synthetic. It has 143 rows: phones 38,
emails 35, addresses 32 and duplicate companies 38. Every telephone number is
in a range reserved for fiction. The rows whose right answer is to hold or
review are 7 phones (plus 1 row where holding and repairing are both
accepted), 8 emails, 4 addresses, and 4 ambiguous duplicate pairs. The
duplicates table also holds 5 hard negative pairs and 18 true duplicate pairs.

The material for each family is the approved starter catalogue item that the
catalogue's own search fixtures name for a query describing the step:
`normalize_phone_numbers`, `normalize_and_recover_email_addresses`,
`split_address_lines_into_components`, and for duplicates both
`find_duplicate_records_with_blocking_keys` and
`score_duplicate_pairs_by_weakest_signal`. Their digests are in the design.

## 6. What the material changed, row by row

Counts are over three repetitions of the cheap model, with the no-material
count first.

- Phones, harm. United Kingdom numbers with a `(0)` trunk prefix: 6 of 6 right
  without the item, 0 of 6 with it; the model kept the 0, as in
  `+4402079460168`. Numbers dialled with the 011 prefix: 6 of 6 against 0 of 6;
  the model held them instead. The area code 015: 3 of 3 against 0 of 3; the
  model added `+1`. The `+415` number with an impossible length: 2 of 3
  against 0 of 3; the model kept it as international. Each of these follows
  the item's written digit-count rule, which does not cover trunk prefixes,
  exit prefixes, area code validity or country number lengths.
- Emails, help and harm in equal measure. Domain endings that cannot exist,
  such as `gmail.con`: 6 of 9 repaired without the item, 9 of 9 with it.
  Domains that could be real, such as `hotmial.com` and `gmail.cm`, which the
  task says to hold: 7 of 9 held without the item, 3 of 9 with it, because the
  model repaired them to `hotmail.com` and `gmail.com`. The item names only
  `gmail.co` as a domain to hold, and the model did not carry that caution to
  similar domains.
- Addresses, a small help. The line whose street and city boundary is
  uncertain, `12 Main St West Springfield MA 01089`, was flagged in 1 of 3
  steps without the item and 3 of 3 with it. Every other row was equal.
- Duplicates, no change in the primary metric. Both arms found 53 or 54 of the
  54 true pair appearances and merged no hard negative. With the items the
  model marked none of the 12 ambiguous pair appearances for review, against
  6 of 12 without them; it left them out as different companies.

## 7. Offered, loaded, used and verified

- Offered: the exact item bytes, bound by digest, were written to `AGENTS.md`
  in the step folder, or after the prompt in the optional arm.
- Loaded: the proxy found the first line of every selected item in every
  request of every material step, 90 requests in all, and in no request of a
  no-material step.
- Used, inferred: words that only the items introduce, such as
  `digit_count_unexpected`, `unrecoverable_shape` and `weakest`, appeared in
  the cheap model's own text or tool arguments in 8 of its 12 material steps
  (phones 3, emails 3, duplicates 2, addresses 0) and in 0 of its 12
  no-material steps. A match is a sign of use, not proof.
- Verified: only the scorer's results in section 5 count as outcomes.

## 8. Failures and disputed rows

- The local model's 37 replies held no structured tool call. 34 held a tool
  call written as JSON text, 10 of them inside a Markdown code fence, and 3
  held other text. Its largest prompt was 2,816 tokens, far below its context
  size, so context truncation did not cause this.
- One truth row is disputed. The frozen truth expects `45 Kingsway Burnaby` to
  be flagged for review, because the street has no type and there are no
  commas. All 9 cloud steps split it as house number 45, street `Kingsway` and
  city `Burnaby`, which a reader who knows that street would accept. Removing
  the row would raise every cloud step by the same 1 of 32 and change no
  comparison.
- The ambiguous duplicate pair of Keystone Plumbing at two addresses could be
  read as two locations, which the task says not to list. This affects only
  the secondary hold rate, not pair F1.
- The model actions were scanned: no step read the truth files, a parent
  folder or the repository, and no step installed a package. Every absolute
  path a model wrote was inside its own step folder.

## 9. Limitations

- The data is synthetic, 143 rows, written by the author of the study, who
  also knew the items. Hard rows include cases where the item's method is
  wrong; they are standard telephone and address conventions, but their
  number shapes the size of the phone result.
- One harness, one local model and two cloud models of one vendor family were
  tested. Three repetitions can only show large, consistent differences.
- The cloud models were near the ceiling without material, which left little
  room to show help.
- The local model's failure belongs to this model, this Ollama version's
  handling of its output and Pi's structured tool interface. Other local
  models, or a harness that reads tool calls from text, were not tested.
- Cloud cost is a subscription and is not metered for each request. Token
  counts are provider-reported.
- Only the catalogue's own search-fixture selection was tested. A combined
  selection, for example adding `apply_hold_or_escalate_each_correction`, was
  not.

## 10. What may and may not be claimed publicly

May be stated, each with a link to this report:

- Baltor measured its first library items against a no-item arm with an
  independent checker, and published the result, including an item that made
  results worse.
- Each step ran in a fresh, isolated harness process that held only its own
  material, and the item text reached every request of the material arms.
- In this frozen synthetic test, `glm-5.3-flash:cloud` without added material
  scored between 0.914 and 1.000 on each of 12 steps.

Must not be stated from this run:

- that Baltor material improves accuracy, lets a small or cheap model match a
  larger one, saves tokens, time or money, or makes overnight local work
  possible;
- any percentage improvement, or any result for a population, model or
  harness that was not tested.

## 11. What this means for the roadmap

These are recommendations for the owning sessions. This run changed no
roadmap entry, catalogue item or review record.

- S-6.47: record a measured negative effect for `normalize_phone_numbers` on
  this population in its review record. A revised item should name its limits:
  trunk prefixes, exit prefixes such as 011, area codes that start with 0 or 1,
  and country number lengths. It needs independent review and a new
  measurement. The email item should say that a plausible real domain is held,
  not only `gmail.co`.
- D-07: `qwen2.5-coder:7b` cannot drive Pi's tools through Ollama's
  OpenAI-compatible interface. Overnight local work needs a local model with
  working structured tool calls. Downloading one needs the owner, because the
  D-07 authority note does not cover installing weights.
- S-6.37: a data cleanup demonstration needs a harder population, where the
  no-material arm does not already score near 1.0, and a model that fails
  without help. The proxy, the isolation recipe, the scorer and the freeze and
  amendment records can be reused as they are.
