## Before posting

- Check the price and the Agent Feeds date on <https://baltor.ai/pricing>.
- X turns words such as `AGENTS.md` into links because `.md` is a domain
  ending. The thread avoids them; keep it that way when editing.
- Post the thread by hand. Do not schedule replies or reposts from a tool
  that acts for you.
