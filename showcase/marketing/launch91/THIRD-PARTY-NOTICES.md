This folder contains original source under the repository's MIT licence. It
does not vendor dependency code, binaries, font files, stock images or samples.

| Dependency | Pinned package | Licence and role |
|---|---|---|
| Three.js | `three@0.186.1` | MIT; real-time geometry, materials, camera and GLB export. Preserve `three/LICENSE` if bundling it. |
| Playwright Core | `playwright-core@1.62.1` | Apache-2.0; local browser capture and public signed-out walkthrough. |
| FFmpeg Static | `ffmpeg-static@5.3.0` | Its installed package declares GPL-3.0-or-later. Preserve the binary build's applicable licensing when redistributing it. No executable is copied by this recipe. |
| FFprobe Static | `ffprobe-static@3.1.0` | Its wrapper package declares MIT. The bundled FFprobe executable retains its applicable FFmpeg licensing. No executable is copied by this recipe. |
| Blender | `5.2.1 LTS` qualified locally | GPL software used as a native editor/renderer. No Blender executable is included. |

Original diagrams, geometry and the synthesized score use no third-party
media asset. Marketing copy and real website captures remain associated with
Baltor.AI and the named products. Public Good search screenshots are a view of
metadata, not a blanket licence to redistribute the catalogue's source files.
No actual customer account, private task, payment or generated customer result
appears in the recordings.
