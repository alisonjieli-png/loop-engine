The decision

Decide whether to request a service comparison for a second fulfillment region. Do not authorize a migration from this brief.

Why it might matter here

The fictional context prioritizes fewer order delays while retaining the current ordering service. A regional option may change transport distance, but neither lead time nor integration fit has been measured.

What the scenario supports

  • Two synthetic records describe the same announced region.
  • The example company has a delivery-delay goal.
  • The existing supplier can remain the comparison baseline.
