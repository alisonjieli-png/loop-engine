# Deliver the best available result when the task is blocked

Missing information, an unavailable tool or a failed check does not justify an empty result. Complete every safe and reversible part, label each kind of material, and state the exact limits.

## When to use it

Use it when the literal requested outcome cannot be completed or verified now, and before asking a question, abstaining or ending the work.

## Steps

1. Complete every part of the task that is safe and reversible.
2. Go through the methods that can still add value: analysis of the observed material, explicitly labelled assumptions, scenario or pro forma analysis, synthetic examples, estimates with uncertainty, analogous solutions, reasoning from first principles, supplemental artifacts, a map of the missing components, and concrete further steps.
3. Label every method as completed, not applicable, unavailable, needing authority, or out of resources.
4. Keep each kind of material distinct in the result: observed, derived, assumed, synthetic, estimated and analogous.
5. Never invent a fact, a permission, an independent verification or a completed external effect.
6. Ask the person only when a typed decision can improve the package or can authorize a remaining effect.
7. Publish the useful package before ending the work, together with the unresolved constraint.

## Checks

- At least one useful method was completed.
- The result does not claim that the requested outcome was verified.
- No authority was invented.
- The limits say exactly what is missing and who can supply it.

## Known-wrong example

A database is unreachable, and the agent answers that it cannot help. The empty answer wastes what was possible: the query could be written and checked against the schema file, the expected result shape could be shown with clearly labelled synthetic rows, and the missing access could be named with the person who can grant it. The opposite error is equally wrong: inventing plausible numbers and presenting them as the real query result.

## What to record

- The list of methods with the label for each.
- The delivered material with its kind.
- The exact blocker, the question that was asked, and the safe work that remains.
