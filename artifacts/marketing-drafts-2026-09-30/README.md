# Captioned Baltor marketing drafts

Rendered September 30, 2026 from the source digests in
[verification.json](verification.json). These are downloadable drafts, not a
paid advertisement campaign or an audience-performance study.

- [Thirty-second portrait social video](baltor-30-second-social.mp4), 1080 by
  1920 pixels, with [captions](baltor-30-second-social.srt).
- [Two-minute landscape pitch](baltor-2-minute-pitch.mp4), 1920 by 1080 pixels,
  with [captions](baltor-2-minute-pitch.srt).

Both use H.264 with a browser-compatible pixel format and have no audio track.
The social draft fades between original title cards over an actual render of
the Ashen Wilds demo. It is not recorded gameplay. The pitch presents the
website's ten slides and preserves dated negative comparison results. Neither
claims automatic video recreation or a measured customer benefit.

The [editable sources](../../showcase/marketing/README.md) reuse the existing
website deck, fonts, mark and original demo. No stock footage, TensorArt account,
paid model or music was used. Observed external browser asset requests: zero.
The export checked text bounds, slide visibility, complete decoding, format
and duration. Engineering inspected the captured frames. No Facebook upload,
advertising purchase or monetization eligibility check occurred.

Next production improvements are recorded in the
[delivery plan](../../docs/roadmap/DELIVERY-SEQUENCE.md#marketing-videos-and-the-website-pitch):
actual customer-workflow footage, rights-cleared narration or music when useful,
