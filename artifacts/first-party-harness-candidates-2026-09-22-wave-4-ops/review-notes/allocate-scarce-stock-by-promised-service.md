- Applicability: logistics planner at a distributor or manufacturer; shortage decision for one item and location. Any model or client may read it; model performance is unmeasured.
- Conceptual typed input: `GrossStockPool`, `ReservationLedger`, `OrderQuantityLedger`, `OrderEligibilityAtCutoff`, `OrderPriorityAtCutoff`, `FutureStockReadyPool`, `ServicePriorityPolicy`, `DecisionCutoff`.
- Conceptual typed output: `AllocationProposal` with per-order item, location, hold, release and priority-status decisions; gross-to-free and reservation bridges; time-qualified future pools; conserved incremental quantities; and unresolved ties.
- Effect class: read-only proposal; no reservation, shipment, order change or customer notice.
- Good case: Gross usable on-hand 12, including 4 active units reserved for A, leaves 8 free. A and B each ordered 10, with none fulfilled or cancelled. With both orders item- and location-matched, unheld, releasable and carrying evidenced priority status, A-first priority permits 6 new units for A and 2 for B. A then has 10 including its reservation, B has 2 and an 8-unit shortfall.
- Known-wrong case: A has highest priority but an active order hold; B has matching item and location and is releasable. Giving scarce free stock to A is wrong even though the quantity and timing checks pass. With A's 4 existing reserved units and 8 free units, propose 0 new to A and at most 8 to B; A's 4 remain blocked and cannot be reassigned without separate authority. An unknown hold, release or priority status excludes A from new allocation and blocks a final lower-priority proposal unless policy allows skipping it. Earlier wrong cases remain: do not give A 10 new units as though its 4 reserved units did not count, or count inbound ready on the 12th toward a promise requiring ready stock on the 10th.
- Nearest starter item: `check_a_table_join_before_trusting_it.md` checks keyed row joins; this method allocates conserved scarce quantity under service priority and partial-shipment rules.
- Nearest earlier candidate: `reconcile-inventory-availability` calculates usable stock at a cutoff; this method assigns that stock to competing promises.
- Limits: The customer supplies effective order eligibility, hold and release rules, contract priorities, handling lead times and partial-shipment rules. A confirmed inbound lot remains conditional future supply, never current free stock. Missing per-order eligibility or reservation evidence blocks the proposal. No operational effect is authorized by this text.
- Customer search phrasings: "Which orders get our twelve available units?"; "Show which deliveries we cannot cover without double promising stock."
