What the price does not cover

Your account page shows the usage the service recorded for your connections. That record is the same one the price is based on, so you can check it yourself.

How to stop paying

When payment is open you can cancel from your account page. Cancelling stops the next payment. Files you already downloaded stay on your computer.

Common questions

Do I need a model key to use Baltor?

No. A Baltor key gives access to permitted material. The model calls happen in your own tools, with your own provider account.

What happens to material I already downloaded?

It stays where you saved it. Revoking a key or cancelling stops new requests; it does not recall files that were already delivered.

Is there a free plan?

The part you install is free and open source. Search on our server is free too. Downloads are the measured unit of the paid plan, and an invited account is free while invitations go out in small groups.

Can a team share one account?

Not today. One account holds one person's keys. Shared accounts and team billing are later work.
