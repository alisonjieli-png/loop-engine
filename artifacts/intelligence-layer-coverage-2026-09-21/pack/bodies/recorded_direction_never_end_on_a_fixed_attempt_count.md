## The guidance as recorded

Never end work on a fixed attempt count. When the same failure repeats, change
the approach: quote the failure, narrow the request to the failing part, try
another registered method, or carry the best result forward as provisional
with its findings recorded.

The same direction names the only reasons to stop: a verified result,
exhausted declared authority, a question that only the owner can answer, a
cancellation, or a provider outage recorded so the work can resume. Record
which one it was.

## What it asks a step to do

Three attempts at the same thing is one attempt repeated. The instruction is
to change something between attempts, and to say what changed.

The four listed changes are the registered ones: quote the exact failure back
into the next request, shrink the request to only the part that failed, switch
to a different registered method, or stop and hand over the best result you
have with its findings attached and its status marked provisional.

## Response expected from a step

A step that stops should name which of the five ending reasons applied. A step
that continues should name what it changed since the last attempt. "Tried
again" is not one of the four changes.

## Limits

This is guidance, not permission and not proof. Persisting never enlarges the
authority a step already has. Continuing to work is not authority to repeat an
external effect, spend more, or call a model that was not permitted.
