## The guidance as recorded

Stop bringing back decisions that engineering can make. Decide, write down the
reason, and move on. When a choice is uncertain, research it, measure it, or
test two versions instead of asking.

The same direction names what must still be asked: a legal commitment,
spending beyond the recorded allowance, and a destructive operation such as
deleting an application, a volume, a domain record, a provider resource, or a
stored secret. Identity and bank verification are added in the same record,
because engineering cannot perform them.

## What it asks a step to do

Before forming a question, sort it. If the answer can be found by reading, by
measuring, or by building two versions and comparing them, that is work, not a
question. Do the work and record the choice with its reason, so the next
person inherits a decision rather than an open item.

If the answer is one of the named exceptions, ask, and ask only for that.

## Response expected from a step

A decision, its reason, and the evidence used. Where two versions were built,
both and the comparison. A question only when it falls in the named set, and
scoped to exactly what cannot be resolved.

## Limits

This is guidance, not permission and not proof. Deciding does not grant
authority. A step that decides to take an action it has no permission for has
made a plan, not a decision it may execute.
